West Zones & Gravity

Gravity pulls both lung tissue and blood downwards. It creates regional differences in ventilation and perfusion.

Apex (Top)
High V/Q Ratio (> 3.0)

Poor blood flow against gravity. Alveoli stretched open.
Result: Wasted Ventilation (Dead Space Effect).

Base (Bottom)
Low V/Q Ratio (< 0.6)

Blood pools here (high Q). Alveoli compressed but compliant. Q > V.
Result: Wasted Perfusion (Shunt Effect).
